# ArkPedra Admin — product notes

## Data and jobs

- Owner: the marmoraria owner or commercial manager, usually on desktop.
- Frequent job: change a price or replace a photograph and confirm that the site is current.
- Objects: 4 stones, 13 price options, 4 editorial image slots.
- Units: R$/m², R$/item, centimetres, R$/linear metre and R$/order.
- Main action: save the row being edited.
- Product-specific object: the stone preview is attached to every material row.
- Empty/error state: the affected block explains what failed and leaves the other sections usable.

## Direction

- Sheet: Accent ladder, seed 576381285.
- Brand face: mineral-green tint ladder from ArkPedra; full green only on small marks and state.
- Shell: open plane, 240px labelled sidebar, no top bar on desktop.
- Screen: settings rows with context and image on the left, editable panel on the right.
- Phone: sidebar folds into a compact header and horizontal section navigation.

## Tokens

- Ground: #f4f7f3
- Surface: #ffffff
- Ink: #161a16

The panel deliberately avoids KPI cards, charts, icon tiles and dashboard vanity numbers. Its density comes from the real pricing table and image previews.
